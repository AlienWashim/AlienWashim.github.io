Enhancing the Tree-Based Keyphrase Extraction Technique Using Embedding Technique

Authors: Md. Washim Akram, Prof. Dr. Md. Saiful Azad, et al.

Abstract / Contribution: Keyphrase extraction is a fundamental task in Information Retrieval and Natural Language Processing. Traditional tree-based hierarchical extraction frameworks often overlook deeper contextual relationships between candidate phrases. This paper introduces an augmented methodology integrating dense embedding representations into tree-based parse graphs, yielding significant performance improvements in identifying salient keywords across unstructured corpora.

Venue: 2024 6th International Conference on Sustainable Technologies for Industry 5.0 (STI 2024), Green University of Bangladesh.
Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Md. Saiful Azad, Professor & Dean of the Faculty of Science and Engineering (FSE).

IEEE Xplore ICCMA 2024 Conference
2024

A Raspberry Pi Driven Autonomous Humanoid Robot for Advanced Applications

Authors: Md. Washim Akram, ASM Ahsanul Sarkar Akib, et al.

Abstract / Contribution: Presents the architecture and algorithmic pipeline of an autonomous humanoid robotic platform built on single-board computer architecture (Raspberry Pi). The system integrates multi-sensor telemetry, inverse kinematics, real-time motor control loops, and environmental feedback to deliver reliable stability, navigation, and human-assistive capabilities in complex dynamic terrains.

Venue: 12th International Conference on Control, Mechatronics and Automation (ICCMA 2024), sponsored by Brunel University London, UK.
Affiliation: Robo Tech Valley | Supervisor: ASM Ahsanul Sarkar Akib (MD & CEO).
